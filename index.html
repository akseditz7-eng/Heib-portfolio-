HEIB is an early-stage concept project exploring distinctive vehicle design, electric performance ideas, and future personalization technology. We are starting lean, learning in public, and developing one concrete deliverable at a time.

Concept artwork only. HEIB PULSE is not a production vehicle; visuals and specifications are provisional and unvalidated.

Why HEIB

Born to outperform.

Our vision is to explore cars that feel expressive, purposeful and attainable over time. We want the design to be recognizable without copying established automakers, and we want every performance claim to be earned through engineering rather than marketing.

Our current stage: concept development

HEIB is not yet a funded manufacturer and has no production vehicle, validated performance figures, or confirmed launch date. We are building a design portfolio and looking for collaborators who want to contribute voluntarily to a clearly scoped early-stage project.

First vehicle concept

HEIB PULSE

A luxury-aggressive electric performance sedan concept: planted proportions, predator-inspired lighting, clean body surfacing, and a restrained metallic palette. Performance goals remain research questions until supported by calculations, simulation and testing.

Design explorations

One identity, multiple details.

These boards communicate the current direction—not finished engineering. We are seeking a designer to unify proportions, details and views into one consistent design package.

Predator Eyes

Three-claw daytime-running-light motif intended to become a recognizable HEIB signature. Final lighting must meet applicable road regulations.

Wide, purposeful rear

A full-width lighting idea, muscular rear shoulders and a diffuser-inspired lower treatment, subject to aerodynamic and packaging review.

Wheel & brake study

Dark multi-spoke wheels with champagne-gold accents and a performance brake concept. Sizes, materials and stopping capability are not validated.

Driver-focused cockpit

Digital displays, tactile controls, dark materials and warm metallic accents; proposed interfaces would be prototyped before any vehicle integration.

Performance ambition

An exploration of a more extreme visual direction. Any horsepower, acceleration or top-speed figures in concept art are fictional targets unless independently validated.

Future exploration

HEIB MORPH

A proposed personalization platform spanning digital themes and, eventually, physical customization. The first zero-budget prototype would be a browser-based visual configurator: choose paint colors, wheel finishes, accent trims and cabin themes, then preview the combination on a concept image. Replaceable exterior panels are a future research idea, not a current product promise.
